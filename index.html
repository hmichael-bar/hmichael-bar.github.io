OFAC sanctions screening engine with Arabic-name matching

Problem: Arabic na

the SDN list are spelled many ways. Exact matching misses real hits, and loose fuzzy matching floods analysts with false positives.

A name-screening engine run against the live Treasury SDN and Consolidated lists. It scores each candidate match from 0 to 100, gives the reasons, and suggests one of the three L1 dispositions: true match, potential match (escalate) or false positive. A dedicated Arabic module treats Moammar el-Gaddafi, Muammar QADHAFI and معمر القذافي as the same name.

FC-06 · KYC / EDDView codeComplete

Adverse media screener in English and Arabic

Problem: Reporting on Gulf, Levant

orth African subjects often appears first, or only, in Arabic. English-only negative-news checks miss it.

Searches GDELT and Google News in both languages. Tags stories across six financial-crime categories, works out the legal stage (allegation, charge or conviction/designation), scores severity, merges syndicated copies, and writes a sourced rating of high, medium, low or none.
